Task Context ngspice/tran.v(v(g_ah)

Develop a production-intent LibrePCB design for one PCI Express Gen 5 x1 host-board channel operating at 32 GT/s.

The channel spans an exact PCIe Gen 5 root-complex, switch, retimer, or FPGA package to a PCIe CEM edge connector or approved board-to-board connector. The host transmit pair contains discrete AC-coupling capacitors. The host receive pair does not contain another series coupling capacitor because AC coupling belongs at the transmitting side of each link direction.

The project focuses on the physical discontinuity created by the transmit AC-coupling capacitor and its land pattern. The baseline production footprint uses a 220 nF capacitor in an 0201 package. A separate characterization coupon shall compare 0201 and 0402 patterns. Alternate footprints shall not be placed in series or as branching DNP pads on the production lane.

The final routing shall use 85-ohm differential impedance, controlled reference planes, symmetric transitions, smooth pad tapers, solver-driven local plane relief, short residual via stubs, and no permanent branch stubs.

External field-solver and signal-integrity tools may be used to validate the completed LibrePCB geometry and to generate Touchstone, TDR, step-response, and eye results. The acceptance values are project targets and must be reconciled with the exact platform and licensed PCIe requirements before a compliance claim is made.
