Subject: Qucs-S PCIe Gen 5 AC-Coupling Channel and DFE Model

Hi Team,

Please develop and validate a reusable Qucs-S model of one PCIe Gen 5 differential channel operating at 32 GT/s NRZ. Build the project from scratch using editable Qucs-S components, mixed-mode ports, imported Touchstone blocks, equations, transient analyses, measurements, and saved plots.

Use these baseline parameters:

- Data rate: 32 GT/s
- Unit interval: 31.25 ps
- Nyquist frequency: 16 GHz
- Differential impedance: 85 ohms
- PCB channel length: 125 mm
- AC-coupling value: 220 nF per conductor
- Baseline capacitor package: 0201
- Comparison package: 0402
- Nominal transmitter differential swing: 800 mV peak-to-peak
- Nominal transmitter 20% to 80% transition time: 12 ps
- Frequency sweep: 10 MHz to 32 GHz
- Sweep resolution: at least 1,601 points

Model the complete electrical path:

TX differential source
-> transmitter package and BGA escape
-> controlled-impedance PCB transmission line
-> AC-coupling capacitor land pattern
-> remaining PCB transmission line
-> receiver package
-> 85-ohm differential receiver termination

Use separate positive and negative conductors and retain four-port data until mixed-mode conversion is performed. Include conductor skin effect, copper roughness, dielectric loss, frequency-dependent phase delay, pad capacitance, capacitor ESL and ESR, via and launch parasitics, and package loss. Document the dielectric Dk, Df, copper thickness, roughness model, and every S-parameter reference plane.

Create or import four-port S-parameter wrappers for:

1. Ideal coupling capacitor
2. Optimized 0201 capacitor and pad pattern
3. Conventional 0402 capacitor and pad pattern
4. Optimized 0201 pattern with reference-plane relief
5. Manufacturing-tolerance variants

Do not represent the land pattern as only an ideal series capacitor. The wrapper shall include both pads, neck-down transitions, local reference-plane geometry, capacitor parasitics, and any via or layer transition included in the physical pattern.

Perform mixed-mode S-parameter analysis and report:

- Sdd21 differential insertion loss
- Sdd11 differential return loss
- Scd21 differential-to-common-mode conversion
- Group delay
- Phase linearity
- Near-end reflection
- 0201 versus 0402 discontinuity difference

Generate a differential TDR or equivalent time-domain impedance plot using the measured or simulated S-parameter data. Identify the transmitter launch, capacitor pads, layer transitions, receiver launch, and termination. Separate the local capacitor discontinuity from the distributed line impedance.

Perform a differential step-response analysis using the specified transmitter edge. Report overshoot, undershoot, ringing frequency, settling time, and post-cursor amplitudes for the ideal, 0201, 0402, and plane-relief cases.

Extract the impulse and pulse response at 32 GT/s. Use a PRBS31 or an equivalently stressful documented sequence to synthesize at least 32,768 unit intervals. Generate eye diagrams for:

- Unequalized channel
- Channel with a documented transmitter FFE preset
- Channel with a five-tap post-cursor DFE
- Optimized 0201 channel with FFE and DFE
- Conventional 0402 channel with the same equalization

Implement a behavioral five-tap DFE. Use one-unit-interval-spaced taps and determine the tap coefficients from the sampled pulse response or a documented optimization method. Limit the DFE coefficients to physically reasonable receiver values and report every coefficient. Prevent the DFE block from using future symbols.

Run these cases:

1. Ideal capacitor channel
2. Conventional 0402 pad channel
3. Optimized 0201 pad channel
4. Optimized 0201 channel with plane relief
5. Minimum and maximum capacitor value
6. Minimum and maximum pad and antipad dimensions
7. Low-loss and high-loss dielectric corners
8. Minimum and maximum copper roughness
9. Nominal and worst-case transmitter edge rate
10. Unequalized, FFE-only, DFE-only, and FFE-plus-DFE receiver cases

Measure and report:

- Sdd21 at 8 GHz and 16 GHz
- Worst Sdd11 from 100 MHz to 16 GHz
- Worst Scd21 from 100 MHz to 16 GHz
- Differential group-delay variation
- Peak local TDR deviation at the capacitor
- Step-response overshoot and settling time
- Main cursor and pre/post-cursor amplitudes
- DFE tap coefficients
- Residual post-cursor ISI energy
- Eye height in millivolts
- Eye width in unit intervals
- Jitter and noise assumptions used in eye synthesis

Project acceptance targets:

- Optimized 0201-channel Sdd21 loss is no greater than 12 dB at 16 GHz.
- Optimized 0201-channel Sdd11 remains at or below -10 dB from 100 MHz to 16 GHz.
- Optimized 0201-channel Scd21 remains at or below -20 dB from 100 MHz to 16 GHz.
- Distributed channel TDR remains between 80.75 ohms and 89.25 ohms outside identified package and capacitor discontinuities.
- The optimized 0201 pad pattern reduces peak capacitor-region TDR deviation by at least 30% relative to the conventional 0402 pattern.
- The optimized 0201 land pattern improves the worst capacitor-region return-loss notch by at least 1.5 dB relative to the 0402 pattern.
- Differential step response settles within +/-5% of its final value in no more than 5 UI.
- Five-tap DFE reduces post-cursor ISI energy by at least 50% relative to the unequalized channel.
- The optimized 0201 channel with equalization provides at least 100 mV eye height and 0.35 UI eye width under the documented assumptions.
- All imported S-parameter blocks use consistent port order, reference impedance, reference planes, and frequency units.
- All saved analyses complete without fatal convergence errors or unexplained floating nodes.

Please submit:

- pcie_gen5_ac_channel.sch
- pcie_gen5_ac_channel.dpl
- pcie_gen5_ac_channel.dat
- all imported Touchstone and local model files
- mixed_mode_sparameters.png
- capacitor_tdr_comparison.png
- differential_step_response.png
- pulse_response_and_isi.png
- eye_unequalized.png
- eye_dfe_comparison.png
- tolerance_summary.csv
- summary.txt

The summary shall document topology, port order, reference impedance, channel length, capacitor models, land-pattern geometry, material parameters, loss models, equalization method, DFE coefficients, eye assumptions, measured results, warnings, convergence settings, and model limitations.

The project is complete when another reviewer can open the Qucs-S project, resolve every local model through relative paths, rerun every saved analysis, regenerate all plots, and reproduce the S-parameter, TDR, step-response, ISI, DFE, and eye results without manually repairing the project.
