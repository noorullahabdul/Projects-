LM317 GEL CHARGER - NODE AND PROBE MAP
======================================

Actual electrical net labels
----------------------------
vin_in             Input supply positive and LM317 IN
vout_bat_pos       LM317 OUT, output capacitor positive, battery positive
adj_ctrl           LM317 ADJ, R4 lower node, R_POT upper node, red LED anode
pot_r3_junction    Junction between R_POT and R3
green_led_anode    Junction between R1 and green LED D1
bat_return         Battery negative, output capacitor return, R3 return, Q1 base
red_led_cathode    Junction between red LED D2 and PR_RED
red_probe_out      Junction between PR_RED and R_RED
lim_collector      Junction between R_RED and Q1 collector
sense_res_top      Junction between PR_CHG and the 1 ohm current-sense resistor
0                  Circuit ground, LM317 reference return, Q1 emitter return

Dedicated current probes
------------------------
PR_CHG              Total charge-return current through R_SENSE
PR_RED              Red-LED/current-limit branch current
