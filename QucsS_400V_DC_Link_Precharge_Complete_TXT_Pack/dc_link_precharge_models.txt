* dc_link_precharge_models.txt
* Qucs-S / ngspice switch-model library
* File extension intentionally kept as .txt; ngspice accepts it through .include.
*
* Electrical contact models
* Control terminals are the third and fourth nodes of each S-device.
*
.MODEL SW_PRECHARGE SW(
+ RON=20m
+ ROFF=1G
+ VT=2.5
+ VH=0.2
+)

.MODEL SW_MAIN SW(
+ RON=20m
+ ROFF=1G
+ VT=2.5
+ VH=0.2
+)

.MODEL SW_DISCHARGE SW(
+ RON=100m
+ ROFF=1G
+ VT=2.5
+ VH=0.2
+)

.MODEL SW_SHORT_FAULT SW(
+ RON=20m
+ ROFF=1G
+ VT=2.5
+ VH=0.2
+)

* Optional command-to-contact buffer.
* IN is the controller command; OUT is the electrical control node.
* Delay and bounce are intentionally implemented with PWL sources in each case,
* because the basic ngspice SW model does not model mechanical travel or bounce.
.SUBCKT CONTACT_BUFFER IN OUT
EOUT OUT 0 VALUE={V(IN)}
ROUT OUT 0 1G
.ENDS CONTACT_BUFFER
