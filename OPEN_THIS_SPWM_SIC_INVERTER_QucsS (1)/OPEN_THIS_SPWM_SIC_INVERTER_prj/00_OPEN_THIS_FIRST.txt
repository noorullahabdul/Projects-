OPEN THIS FILE IN QUCS-S
========================

OPEN:
  OPEN_THIS_SPWM_SIC_INVERTER.sch

DO NOT OPEN:
  BUCK_Converter_V1_REAL_SYMBOLS.sch

This project is the three-phase SPWM SiC inverter:
  750 V DC link
  40 kHz SPWM
  50 Hz output
  Three dual gate drivers
  Six SiC switches
  Three-phase LC filter and star load

Run:
  1. Select ngspice.
  2. Open OPEN_THIS_SPWM_SIC_INVERTER.sch.
  3. Press F2.
