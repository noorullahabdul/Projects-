This bundle combines user-uploaded circuit designs, prepared working copies and
manufacturer symbols/models recovered from the user's LTspice installer.
Manufacturer models remain under their original licences and are not claimed
as original user or assistant code. See vendor_reference/License.txt.

No blanket open-source licence is applied to the uploaded designs or vendor
models. Their publication/redistribution rights were not independently verified.
Keep vendor attribution and review applicable project agreements before sharing.

Static checks and an ideal behavioral ADC simulation do not establish hardware
performance or production/safety acceptance of the five circuits.
