AUTOMOTIVE 12 V INPUT-PROTECTION FRONT END
Complete Design Document
=========================================

1. Purpose
----------
This design protects a low-power automotive electronic module from:
  - Normal 12 V battery operation
  - 16 V high-battery operation
  - -14 V reverse battery
  - 60 V load dump

The circuit includes a fuse resistance, bidirectional TVS clamp, L-C-L input
filter, high-side P-channel MOSFET reverse-polarity stage, gate-source zener,
100 uF bulk capacitor, 10 uF high-frequency output capacitor, and 12 ohm load.

2. Final topology
-----------------
Battery source
  -> source impedance
  -> 50 mOhm fuse resistance
  -> VIN_RAW

At VIN_RAW:
  -> bidirectional 24 V-class TVS to GND
  -> L1, 10 uH with 20 mOhm DCR

VIN_FILT:
  -> CIN, 2.2 uF to GND
  -> L2, 10 uH with 20 mOhm DCR

VIN_PROT:
  -> Q1 drain

Q1:
  Drain  -> VIN_PROT
  Source -> VOUT_PROT
  Gate   -> GATE

Gate network:
  RSG  = 1 MegOhm from VOUT_PROT to GATE
  RGND = 100 kOhm from GATE to GND
  DZGS = 12 V zener, anode at GATE and cathode at VOUT_PROT

At VOUT_PROT:
  -> CBULK, 100 uF / 50 V with 80 mOhm ESR
  -> COUT, 10 uF / 50 V with 30 mOhm ESR
  -> RLOAD, 12 Ohm to GND

3. Why the PMOS orientation is correct
--------------------------------------
The PMOS body diode is oriented from VIN_PROT to VOUT_PROT.

Positive battery:
  The body diode initially charges the output.
  The source voltage rises while the gate remains low.
  VGS becomes negative and Q1 turns on with low resistance.

Reverse battery:
  The body diode is reverse biased.
  VOUT_PROT remains near ground through the load.
  GATE also remains near ground.
  VGS remains near 0 V, so Q1 remains off.

4. Gate-network calculation
---------------------------
Before the zener conducts:

  VGATE = VSOURCE x RGND / (RSG + RGND)

With:
  RSG = 1 MegOhm
  RGND = 100 kOhm

  VGATE = 0.0909 x VSOURCE

At VSOURCE = 12 V:

  VGATE approximately 1.09 V
  VGS = VGATE - VSOURCE
      approximately -10.91 V

At VSOURCE = 16 V, the divider would request:

  VGS approximately -14.55 V

The 12 V zener therefore begins clamping and prevents excessive negative VGS.

During reverse battery:

  VSOURCE approximately 0 V
  VGATE approximately 0 V
  VGS approximately 0 V

5. Load calculations
--------------------
At 12 V:

  ILOAD = 12 V / 12 Ohm = 1.0 A
  PLOAD = 12^2 / 12 = 12 W

At 16 V:

  ILOAD = 16 V / 12 Ohm = 1.33 A
  PLOAD = 16^2 / 12 = 21.33 W

The load resistor is an ideal simulation load unless a power rating is added
for documentation.

6. Filter calculation
---------------------
For either 10 uH inductor with the 2.2 uF shunt capacitor:

  f0 = 1 / [2*pi*sqrt(L*C)]
     approximately 33.9 kHz

Explicit 20 mOhm inductor DCR and capacitor ESR provide first-order damping.
This simulation does not validate a complete automotive conducted-EMI filter.

7. TVS model and load-dump assumption
-------------------------------------
The TVS is a functional bidirectional 24 V-class subcircuit.

Approximate behavior:
  Stand-off region: negligible current at 12 V, 16 V, and -14 V
  Avalanche begins near 26.5 V plus the forward diode drop
  Dynamic resistance: approximately 250 mOhm per avalanche element
  Intended clamp range: approximately 30 V to 34 V in the simulated surge range

Load-dump source:
  Initial voltage: 12 V
  Peak voltage: 60 V
  Rise time: 1 ms
  Hold time: 100 ms
  Decay time: 100 ms
  Source resistance: 1 Ohm

The TVS electrical clamp can be validated, but the functional model does not
prove real-device pulse-energy survival.

8. Capacitor ratings
--------------------
CBULK:
  100 uF / 50 V

COUT:
  10 uF / 50 V

The 50 V rating is required because the protected rail may approach the
30 V to 36 V clamp region during load dump.

9. Expected results
-------------------
Nominal 12 V:
  VOUT_PROT should be at least 11 V.
  TVS current should be negligible.
  PMOS VGS should be negative and within the zener-clamped limit.

High battery 16 V:
  VOUT_PROT should remain functional.
  TVS current should remain negligible.
  Gate-source zener may begin limiting VGS.

Reverse battery -14 V:
  VOUT_PROT magnitude should remain below 1 V.
  Load current should remain negligible.
  PMOS VGS should remain near 0 V.

Load dump 60 V:
  VIN_RAW and VOUT_PROT should clamp below 36 V.
  TVS current and power should rise during the pulse.
  PMOS VGS should remain limited by DZGS.

10. Measurement expressions
----------------------------
VGS:
  V(GATE,VOUT_PROT)

VDS:
  V(VIN_PROT,VOUT_PROT)

Input current:
  I(V_IIN)

Load current:
  I(RLOAD)

TVS current:
  I(V_ITVS)

TVS power:
  V(VIN_RAW) x I(V_ITVS)

12. Acceptance criteria
-----------------------
[ ] VOUT_PROT >= 11 V at 12 V input
[ ] Functional output at 16 V input
[ ] |VOUT_PROT| <= 1 V at -14 V input
[ ] Reverse load current is negligible
[ ] Load-dump VOUT_PROT peak < 36 V
[ ] Peak ITVS is measured
[ ] Peak PTVS is measured
[ ] PMOS VGS remains within the documented clamp range
[ ] PMOS VDS remains below 60 V
[ ] No fatal convergence error occurs

13. Limitations
---------------
This model does not validate:
  - Actual fuse opening
  - TVS pulse-energy survival
  - MOSFET thermal impedance or SOA
  - ISO 7637-2 certification waveforms
  - Wiring-harness inductance
  - PCB parasitic inductance
  - EMC compliance
  - Component tolerance and temperature corners
